Connect your farm

A small browser extension reads your Farm RPG pages while you play and hands them to this planner. It never clicks or plays for you, and nothing leaves your computer. About five minutes, once. Works in Chrome, Brave and Edge on a computer.

  1. Open the planner

    Either keep using it right here on the website, alikdash1.github.io/Farmrpgcalculator, which needs nothing installed, or download the whole planner, unzip it and double-click index.html. The download runs from your own computer, offline, and starts empty.

  2. Add the extension

    Download the extension

    Unzip it and put the farm-rpg-account-sync folder somewhere it can stay. The browser loads it from there, so don't delete it afterwards. It is also inside the full planner download.

    Go to chrome://extensions (brave://extensions or edge://extensions), switch on Developer mode in the corner, press Load unpacked and choose that folder.

    Using the downloaded index.html? Press Details on the extension and switch on Allow access to file URLs.

  3. Visit your pages

    Keep the planner open in one tab. In another, open Farm RPG and refresh it once, then visit your Profile, Inventory, Tower, Mastery, Perks, Quests and My Farm. This page then says Extension connected and your farm fills in: inventory, perks and their tiers, Tower and quests. From then on it keeps up as you play.

    Still waiting? Press the reload arrow on the extension's card, then reload this page.

What the extension can see

Only these sites, which Details on the extension lists too:

  • https://farmrpg.com/*the game pages it reads
  • https://www.farmrpg.com/*the same site
  • file:///*the downloaded planner, only if you switch on file access
  • http://localhost/*the planner, run on your own computer
  • http://127.0.0.1/*the same
  • https://alikdash1.github.io/Farmrpgcalculator/*this planner's website
