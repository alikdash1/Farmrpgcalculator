## Refinement pass — 26 August 2026

- Removed the Workshop Economics / Craft and Sell card.

  the automatic fastest route.

  excluded by default and require the seasonal-location switch.

  page captures, which are validated and merged locally in the tab.
- Account review shows player/Tower, inventory, consumables, quests, masteries,
  warnings and missing fields.
- Applying account data remains explicit. Only exact item names and bonuses
  explicitly marked owned are applied.
- Flattened the background and reduced route-color competition.

## Honest remaining gaps

- The Calculator still relies on the calculator's curated static engine data;
  the Library export provides mechanics/rules/coverage, not all 1,360 database
  entities as a second runtime item engine.
- Most account pages outside Inventory still use provisional capture parsing.
- Captured-but-unparsed research sources, thin quest rewards and missing
  structured Sawmill/Quarry/Storehouse data remain knowledge gaps.
- The Account tab can review and safely apply confirmed values, but it does not
  yet turn quest/Tower/mastery state into a complete next-action progression
  advisor. That remains the next major phase.
