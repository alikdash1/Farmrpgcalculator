Apple cider  is a click-reducing item that allows you to explore many times with a single click. Without any perks or upgrades it explores 1010 times and cannot be used unless you have at least 1010 stamina, but this number can be increased with perks and 
Exploring Effectiveness upgrades.
To help offset its crafting cost of 40 apples , cider also increases drop rates in most zones.

Perks and Meals
Apple cider benefits from a number of perks and meal effects:

Cinnamon Sticks increases your cider explore count by 25%, to a minimum of 1263. This also increases the minimum stamina required to use a cider and the average stamina consumed when using one.
Wanderer perks reduce the amount of stamina used by cider in the same way that they reduce stamina use when manually exploring, up to a maximum of 33%.
Sprint Shoes perks increase your exploring effectiveness, which in turn increases your cider explore count. See below for more info.
Neigh  decreases the stamina used when exploring with cider by 20%, stacking multiplicatively with Wanderer for a maximum reduction of 46.4%.
Cabbage stew  allows you to use 5 ciders per click, speeding up exploration even more.

Drop Rate Buff
When exploring manually, the zones around Redbrook have different 
Zone Drop Rates. These rates determine how many items you find (on average) each time you explore. These range from 0.25 (1 item for every 4 explores) to 0.40 (1 item for every 2.5 explores). Cider sets this value to 0.40 in every zone.

This is usually a buff, and has the largest effect on zones with lower zone drop rates like Highland Hills, Whispering Creek, and Jundland Desert.

Exploring Effectiveness
Your 
Exploring Effectiveness in each zone also increases your cider explore count. Before any perks or upgrades, each point of exploring effectiveness will grant an additional 10 explores. With Cinnamon Sticks, this increases to 12.5 explores (rounded as needed). This also increases the minimum stamina required to use a cider, and the average stamina consumed when using one.
